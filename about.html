My Story

I grew up in Poblacion, Tubod, in a close-knit family of six. My mother is a devoted housewife who keeps our home together, and my father is a government employee who works hard to provide for us. Together with my three younger siblings, we make up a household full of love, lessons, and laughter.

As the eldest child, I carry a responsibility that comes with both pride and pressure. My siblings look up to me, so I strive to be the example they can follow in my words, my work, and my character. That responsibility pushes me to persevere, because I want to build a better future and improve the quality of life for my family.

Being a graduating student is not easy. Settling on a thesis topic was one of my hardest decisions, and I went through several ideas before narrowing down. Also, the remaining subjects that I need to take before graduating also plays a crucial role for my graduating status. These experiences taught me to be patient, to ask questions, and to keep going even when the path is unclear.

Outside academics, I love rock music and singing, and I play pickleball for fun. These keep me balanced, because mining engineering demands focus and I need outlets that recharge me.
